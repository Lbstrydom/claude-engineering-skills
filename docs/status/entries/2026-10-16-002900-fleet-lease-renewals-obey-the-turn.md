## 2026-10-16 — `fleet lease`: renewals obey the turn, a live own slot is not rotated, sync:pr leases as the source repo (PR 5 of the fleet-lease theme, #265)

**Why.** Three incidents from the evening of 2026-10-15, all read from the lease journal. (1) Turn starvation (20ec): wine's turn ended on time (`turn-ended expiry`, 20:23:37, 60 minutes after its first grant), but `feat/wine-receiving-placement-pr2a` re-acquired its own slot without a token ("rotated") every ~90 s; a renewal or rotation is `basis: same-job` and skipped the turn rule, so the holder kept the SLOT while storyline waited 94+ refusals, and each rotation also closed two sibling wine jobs' waits as "served". (2) Same-name takeover (8182): at 18:45Z a worker named like storyline's coordinator rotated its CI-hold token, and the worker's release freed the slot mid-CI. (3) sync:pr (99ea) leased as the TARGET repo, so a CES sync competed with the target's own workers inside the target's turns and was deferred ~90 minutes.

- **D11** (`lease.mjs` `decideAcquire`): a renewal, or the rotation of an EXPIRED own slot, is judged by the same `turnRule` as a fresh grant, on a projection of the settled lease with only the continuing job's own occupancy removed (on the real lease every competing waiter fails admission on the slot being renewed, so backfill would grant it forever - plan audit R1 H1). Granted ones stay `same-job`: no turn starts, the 60-minute budget counts from the turn's first grant, and the repo's own waiter entry is left alone. After the budget with another repo waiting: refused `turn`, the repo queued, "this lease is not extended - finish and `lease release`"; the job is never cut. `--end-turn` (#260) cannot keep a turn past its budget.
- **D12**: a token-less acquire of a LIVE slot the same identity holds is refused `held` (not queued): pass `--token`, and give a second session of one checkout its own `--id`. An expired own slot can still be taken back; a matching-token renewal is unchanged.
- **D13** (`sync-pr.mjs`, `lease-run.mjs`, `decideReleaseToCiWindow`): the lease identity is the source repo (the CES checkout the script lives in) with holder `sync-pr:<target>`, so a sync takes CES's turns. The CI window it records stays the TARGET's (`windowRepo`, default unchanged): a CES-owned window would exempt every CES job and the next target's sync could stack load on the previous target's CI (plan audit R1 H2).
- **Red first.** The 20:23Z and 18:45Z replays failed against the unmodified `decideAcquire` (3 of 7 D11 tests, the D12 replay, 5 D13 tests). New property invariants I7 (a renewal is granted iff a fresh grant on a free slot would be; same-job; starts no turn; serves no waiter) and I8 (no token-less acquire succeeds on a live own slot), with a `renew` operation and coverage guards. Negative controls: D11's check disabled, I7 fails at seed 1; D12 disabled, I8 fails at seed 12.
